wuapi brand kit
https://wuapi.dev/brand

svg/   vector logos. Use these when you can.
png/   the same logos at fixed sizes, by form and variant.

Forms
  mark              the speech bubble alone
  lockup            mark and wordmark; PNG sizes are heights
  app-icon          the mark on a filled square, for avatars and app icons
  app-icon-rounded  the same with rounded corners

Variants
  color             the default, for dark backgrounds
  color-on-light    for light backgrounds
  white             one color, for dark backgrounds and photos
  black             one color, for light backgrounds and print

Also
  favicon.ico           16, 32 and 48 px
  apple-touch-icon.png  180 px
  og-card.png           1200 x 630 social card
  avatar-500.png        500 px, for GitHub, npm and other profiles

Clear space: keep free on every side at least the gap between the mark and
the wordmark (3/8 of the mark's height). Smallest size: 16 px tall. Don't
stretch, rotate, recolor or add effects to it, and don't place it on a busy
photo.
